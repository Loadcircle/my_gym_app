This Privacy Policy explains how GymVault Team ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use GymVault (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We may collect and store information such as:

  • Account data: name and email address.
  • Profile/fitness data: sex, weight, height, measurements, or other data you enter.
  • Training data: exercises, routines, sets, reps, weights, and progress.

Note: We do not sell your personal information.

3. Services and Providers (Firebase and Others)

We use third-party services, primarily Google Firebase, for:

  • Authentication: Firebase Authentication (email/password, Google, Apple when applicable).
  • Database: Firestore/Database (storage of your progress).
  • Files: Firebase Storage (photos or other files you upload).
  • Analytics: Firebase Analytics (may be enabled, and usage may expand in the future).
  • Errors: Firebase Crashlytics (to detect and fix crashes).

These providers may collect technical data necessary to provide their services (e.g., crash events, installation identifiers).

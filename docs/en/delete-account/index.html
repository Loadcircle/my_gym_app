At GymVault, we respect your right to control your data. You can request the complete deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

From the app

  1. Open the GymVault app.
  2. Go to Settings (from the side menu).
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

By email

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by writing to: jesusmilano96+GymVault@gmail.com

Please include the email address associated with your account in your message so we can verify your identity and process the request.

2. Data That Is Deleted

When you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed, including:

  • Account data: name, email address, and authentication credentials.
  • User profile: sex, weight, height, and any other profile data.
  • Training data: exercise history, weight records, sets, reps, and progress.
  • Custom exercises: any exercises created by you.
  • Routines: all your routines and their items.
  • Routine completions: history of completed routines.
  • Files: profile photos or other files stored in your account.

3. No Data Is Retained

We do not retain any of your personal data after account deletion. The deletion is complete and irreversible. No backups of your data are kept once the request has been processed.

4. Timeframes

When you delete your account from the app, the process is executed immediately. If you submit the request by email, we will process the deletion within a reasonable timeframe after verifying your identity.
